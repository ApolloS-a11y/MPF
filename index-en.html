What exactly does this package cover?

It's basically a package of fixes for the main gameplay aspects of the game, such as cars, customization, crashes, script updates, and much more.

In addition to having improvements that bring a different or even more refined gameplay style compared to the normal version, such as added police cars and speedometer changes,

DXVK (Optimization), Controller Compatibility, and even a 100% Save Game.

The entire V1 Changelog

-Fixed wanted levels 10 and 15, as they had formations that did not exist;

-Fixed issues with SUVs not appearing or taking a long time to appear on levels 8-15;

-Improved support has been created for levels 10 to 15;

-The number of patrol cars on duty at each wanted level has been increased;

-Police radios are now functioning on levels 11-15;

-The mod's NIS Fixes have been updated to an updated version;

-Improved audio quality for police chase music;

-Fixed stutter when disabling music in NFS Undercover;

-Fixed the NFS Undercover music that wasn't changing volume in-game;

-Most existing scripts have been updated for better stability; (Except for Unlimiter, as it removes the randomization of car colors in dealerships and menus.)

-Added MWCrashFix to prevent random crashes during chases;

-Improved mod optimization, resulting in a much lighter load. Despite removing the enbseries, I configured reshade and left it the same as before.

_____________________

The entire V2 Changelog

In addition to the fixes and improvements from V1, we have:

-Added a limit adjuster to prevent invisible cars;

-Fixed an issue where replaying a previously completed race would take much longer to load races and speed cameras, or even cause crashes;

-The number of patrol cars on duty at each wanted level has been increased;

-Added the "Optional" category, which will serve to add more life to the game:

-> Controller Compatibility: Improves compatibility with controllers, making the system more modern, such as: NFS Rivals, 2015, Payback, Heat, and Unbound.

-> The DXVK file has now been moved to the "Optional" folder and is compatible with all Reshade Presets;

-> Modified Speed ​​HUD: You can swap the HUDs for higher quality ones, with 14 options available;

-> Motion Blur Lighter: Reduces motion blur, potentially resulting in a better visual experience;

-> Police Cars Added: This will bring police chases to life, adding a total of 16 patrol cars;
